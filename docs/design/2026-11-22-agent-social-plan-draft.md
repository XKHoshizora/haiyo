# Agent social (batch 3) — plan draft for review

Status: DRAFT, 2026-11-22. Written by the controller (Claude) from decisions made with the product owner today. Nothing here is built. This file is the input for an independent review.

## The product in two paragraphs
Haiyo is one chat site where people and AI Agents are both residents. Every Agent has an owner (a person). Today (deployed): people friend each other and chat 1:1 and in groups; an owner creates Agents (persona, model from the site or the owner's own provider key), talks to each in its own conversation, and may add an Agent to a group the owner is in, where anyone can @ it; Agents in a group can @ each other with brakes (a per-chain points budget, a stop after 30 consecutive Agent messages, a "stop the Agents" button). Agents have memory about their owner ("关于我" notes, a vault, "听说的" hearsay), read-only tools (search this conversation's history, read a file, time, and a web search tool), proactive replies in groups, rolling summaries, owner-only whispers. The owner pays for what the Agent says, in points (1 point = 0.001 USD of upstream list price), from a free daily allowance and a balance; other people's use of an owner's Agent is capped by the owner's daily budget for others (default 20 points).

The vision the owner states: "in the future AIs will socialise with each other like people do, and people and AIs will keep socialising." Batch 3 is the first step where an Agent is reachable outside its owner's own presence.

## Binding principles (from the owner)
- P1 Nobody is ever made to wait for the owner. People do not watch the chat all the time. A confirmation may only come after the fact and must expire by itself. Test: "what happens if the owner does not look at the phone for 8 hours?"
- P2 The Agent never says a canned sentence; anything posted as the Agent is generated by its model. System UI text is fine.
- P3 Good defaults over switches; seamless over confirmation; automatic things act and can be undone.
- P4 A setting shown to users asks ONE plain question with options that state outcomes, not mechanisms. Do not assume users are clever.
- P5 Nothing of memory reaches anyone but the owner unless the rules for that audience allow it; fail toward not sharing. The vault never leaves the owner's own conversation.
- P6 Fix the design, do not patch. Design on paper and have it attacked before code. Measure before choosing.
- P7 Research how mature products do it first, with sources.
- Money: tools are charged like model calls (hold the worst case, charge the actual cost); the free allowance never pays for a paid-tier model; a started reply always finishes; nothing spends without a bound.

## Why would anyone talk to someone else's Agent? (the owner's question; the answer shapes the scope)
1. As the owner's proxy: "Is Lin free on Saturday?", "What does he not eat?" — short, depends on what the Agent may say about its owner to this person.
2. As a persona/tool the owner made and shares (a résumé helper) — may be long, unrelated to the owner.
3. For work, once Agents can act (tasks, a virtual computer, external Agents such as Codex / Claude Code connected in): teammates ask about progress and plans.
4. Agent to Agent: my Agent asks your Agent to find a dinner time.
Today only built-in chat Agents exist, so real demand is mostly 1 and 4, a little of 2. Therefore the batch is deliberately small.

## Decisions already made with the owner
D1 Who may add an Agent — one question in the Agent's settings, 「谁可以加它为好友」, three options: 需要我同意 (DEFAULT: anyone who knows the exact handle may send a request; the owner approves; requests expire after 7 days; cooldown after a refusal) / 我的联系人可以直接加 (the owner's friends are accepted at once; others still need approval; a sub-entry "只让其中几位直接加" picks individuals) / 不让别人加 (invisible: lookup answers exactly like a non-existent handle). Internally two fields (who is auto-accepted; whether others may request). No "public to everyone". Adding = being able to chat 1:1. Lookup by exact handle only (`owner.agentname`), rate-limited; no directory of Agents. The owner's own Agents are connected to each other and to the owner without friending. Narrowing the setting later keeps existing contacts until the owner removes them. Blocking a person already blocks them from the owner's Agents.
D2 In a 1:1 conversation between a person and someone else's Agent, the Agent's owner sees everything (existing product rule: the owner sees every conversation the Agent takes part in) and MAY interject, as themself, marked 主人. These conversations are listed under the Agent ("its conversations with others"), not in the owner's own chat list, with no notifications by default. The owner's interjection does not make the Agent answer the owner.
D3 The other person is ALWAYS told that the owner can see: at request time, in the conversation header ("Agent · 主人 小林 可见"), and by the 主人 mark on interjections. Cannot be switched off. No "owner is reading / has read" indicator.
D4 Who pays (v1): the Agent's OWNER, from the same daily budget that already caps other people's @ in groups — one concept: "others using my Agent, at most N points a day". "The visitor pays" is deferred until Agents do work; the money model keeps "who pays" as a parameter.
D5 Scope (shown to the owner as a picture; awaiting his confirmation):
- Foundation, before anything: (a) the money model rewritten on one rule ("wallet": one hold per run against the owner's pots, split at settlement, allowance first; in progress); (b) memory zones core: for each note × audience a stored yes/no "may be used here", computed in the background; with no confidence the default is no; unfamiliar audiences (a stranger, another owner's Agent) get only the clearly harmless tier at first.
- Ring 1, "others reach your Agent": D1–D4; requests in the contacts page; remove / block; what the Agent may say to this person (memory zones); what the other person says goes only to the Agent's hearsay, never to notes about the owner.
- Ring 2, "Agent to Agent": the owner's own Agents can reach each other; an Agent may add another owner's Agent under that Agent's D1 rule; OWNER-INITIATED only ("ask Zhou's Agent whether he is free on Saturday"), the Agent goes, asks, and brings the result back to its owner in their own conversation; both owners see the Agent↔Agent conversation and may interject; brakes as today (chain budget, stop after 30 consecutive Agent messages, an owner can halt).
- Deferred: adding an Agent to a group its owner is not in; Agent-only groups; automatic multi-round discussions; freezing a conversation and recovery plans (the 2025 prototype's rules — they require approvals from every owner and so conflict with P1; to be redesigned, not postponed as-is); contact groups; invite links; visitor pays; interactive cards (A2UI-style); group files / newcomer history; group nicknames; streaming in groups; external Agents (A2A).

## What exists in the code that this touches (see also `.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-22-polish/batch3-groundwork.md` if present locally; it is not in git)
- `principals.kind` human|agent; an Agent has `owner_id`; handles `owner.name`.
- Friend tables are principal-to-principal; `findByHandle` filters `kind='human'`; Agents are not blockable by id; contacts list shows only one's own Agents.
- Conversations: `direct`, `group`, `agent` (the Agent's own conversation with its owner; `agent_conversations.agent_id` is UNIQUE — one own conversation per Agent). Memberships have `role` speaker|observer but only `speaker` is ever written or read. No freeze state.
- Agent runs: lanes `own` / `others` / `proactive`; `maybeTrigger` returns nothing for `direct`; "anywhere but the Agent's own conversation is a group" (`agents/port-impl.ts`); `notesFor` is binary (own conversation: everything; elsewhere: non-vault notes + that conversation's hearsay).
- Agent-to-Agent in a group: `triggerFromAgent` (halt → chain stop at 30 → chain budget → others' budget), the caller is traced to the person behind the chain.

## Open design questions the controller has not yet put to the owner
Q-a Ring 2 mechanics: how does the owner's instruction become an Agent-to-Agent conversation — a tool the Agent calls ("ask_agent(handle, question)") that posts into a 1:1 conversation between the two Agents, waits (bounded) for the answer, and returns it as a tool result; or an asynchronous errand that reports back later? What if the other Agent's owner's budget is exhausted or the Agent is paused?
Q-b In ring 2, whose points pay for the asked Agent's answer — its owner (others' budget), as in D4?
Q-c What may Agent B tell Agent A about B's owner? (memory zones: audience = "A, the Agent of X, asking on X's behalf".) And what does A remember of what B said (hearsay about B's owner?).
Q-d Prompt injection across owners: B's reply is untrusted text for A (and vice versa). What structural guards beyond "treat as data"?
Q-e Can a person block an Agent (not its owner)? Report abuse?
Q-f Conversation model: a new conversation type for person↔other's Agent and Agent↔Agent, or generalise `direct` to any two principals with the owners as enforced observers (the unused `observer` role)? How do sync/unread/search/attachments/whispers/memory hearsay treat an observer-owner who may speak?
Q-g Does an Agent proactively speak in a 1:1 with a non-owner (e.g. follow up later)? Proposed: no in v1; it only answers.
Q-h Rate limits and abuse for requests and for 1:1 chats with someone's Agent (per-caller per-minute limit exists for group @).
Q-i What does the owner see when their Agent has many conversations with others — a list under the Agent; search; delete?
Q-j Leaving: when the owner removes a contact from the Agent, deletes the Agent, or is blocked by the visitor — what happens to the conversation and to what the Agent heard?

## What the reviewer is asked
Find what is wrong, missing or over-built in this plan: product logic (does each decision survive the principles, especially P1, P4, P5), abuse and privacy, money, the hardest technical risks in the existing code, a better order of work, anything in "deferred" that is actually required for ring 1 or ring 2 to be useful or safe, and anything in rings 1–2 that should be cut. Prefer concrete failure scenarios over general advice.
