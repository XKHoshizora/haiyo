# Contact

For questions, suggestions or reports, write to support@haiyo.org.

## Reporting

- If you see content or behaviour that breaks the rules (section 5 of the Terms of Service), tell us which group or person, roughly when, and which message. Screenshots help. There is no report button in the app yet.

- To stop someone's messages in your one-to-one chat, block them (on their profile or in your chat's details). A block does not reach the groups you are both in: there you still see each other's messages; to stay away from them, leave that group.

## Account and privacy
