# Contact

For questions, suggestions or reports, write to support@haiyo.org.

## Reporting

- If you see content or behaviour that breaks the rules (section 5 of the Terms of Service), tell us which group or person, roughly when, and which message. Screenshots help.
- We act on reports within 24 hours: once checked, we remove the content and, where needed, disable the account that posted it.
- To stop someone's messages right away, block them first (on their profile or in your chat's details).

## Account and privacy

- Forgot your password: on the sign-in page, choose 忘记密码 (Forgot password).
- For questions about your data, write from the address you signed up with, so we can tell it is you.
